Frequently Asked Questions

  1. How do I open a new bank account?

    If you are not yet a customer, you can open an account by visiting any branch or by applying online (if the bank offers that option). You will need to fill out an account opening form and provide the required KYC documents (identity proof, address proof, etc.). The bank staff will verify your documents, and after you make an initial deposit (if required), your account will be opened.

  2. What documents are required to open a bank account?

    To open an account in India, you must provide identity and address proof documents in line with KYC requirements. Typically, the following documents are required: a valid photo ID (such as a passport or Aadhaar card), proof of address (such as a utility bill or driver’s license showing your address), your PAN card (tax identification number), and two recent passport-sized photographs. The bank may ask to see both the originals and photocopies of these documents for verification.

  3. How do I register for the online banking service?

    To register for online banking, you must first have an active bank account. If you already have an account, you can enable online banking by submitting a request at your branch or through the bank’s official website. Typically, you will need to provide your account number, registered mobile number, and other identification details. After verification, the bank will provide you with login credentials (a username and an initial password) for your first login to the system.

  4. How do I log in to my online banking account?

    To log in to online banking, go to the bank’s official website and click on the “Login” option. Then enter your username (user ID) and the password you received during registration or that you have set. For security, you may be required to complete an additional verification step, such as entering a one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone. After successfully entering the required information, you will gain access to your online banking account.

  5. Can I change my password?

    Yes, you can change your password at any time. In the online banking portal’s settings (profile) menu, there is an option to “Change Password.” It is recommended to update your password periodically and choose a strong combination to ensure your account’s security. Note that the new password must meet the bank’s security requirements (for example, a minimum length and inclusion of numbers and special characters).

  6. What should I do if I forget my Internet banking password?

    If you forget your password, use the “Forgot Password?” function on the online banking login page. The system will ask you to confirm personal details (such as your account number or answer a security question) and may send a one-time code to your registered phone or email. By following the on-screen instructions, you can reset your old password and set a new one. If online reset is not available, visit a bank branch for assistance — after verifying your identity, you will be issued new temporary login credentials.

  7. What should I do if I forget my username (login ID)?

    The username is usually provided during registration and is noted in your welcome kit or in a welcome email from the bank. If you have forgotten it, first check the documents given when you set up online banking or any emails from the bank — your login ID might be mentioned there. For security reasons, the online banking system will not display a forgotten username online, so you cannot retrieve it yourself. If you cannot find your login ID, you will need to contact the bank (visit a branch or call customer support) to regain access. You may have to go through an identity verification or re-registration process, after which the bank will issue you new login credentials.

  8. What should I do if I am unable to access my online banking account?

    If you are unable to log in to your account, ensure that you are entering the correct username and password (keeping in mind uppercase and lowercase characters). After multiple incorrect attempts, the system may have temporarily locked your access for security reasons. In that case, wait 1–2 hours and try to log in again, or use the password recovery option. If the problem persists, contact the bank to have your account unlocked — you may need to verify your identity and have your login credentials reset.

  9. How can I change the mobile number I have registered with the bank?

    To change the mobile number linked to your account, you typically need to visit a bank branch in person. You will have to fill out a request form to update your contact information and present a valid ID for verification. The bank may ask for copies of official documents (passport, driver’s license, Aadhaar, etc.) before updating the information. After your request is processed, your new phone number will be linked to your account in the bank’s system.

  10. How do I update my personal details (address, email) with the bank?

    If your residential address or email has changed, you need to notify the bank. To update your address, you will usually have to visit a branch and provide a proof document (for example, a new utility bill or rental agreement showing your new address). In many cases, you can update your email through online banking: in your customer profile there is an option to change the email, and a confirmation code will be sent to the new address. The updated information will take effect after the bank verifies and approves the changes.

  11. Is it mandatory to link my PAN or Aadhaar to my bank account?

    Under India’s KYC and tax regulations, linking your PAN and Aadhaar to your bank account is mandatory. If you did not provide these details when opening the account, the bank may request them later within a specified timeframe; otherwise, your account functionality might be restricted. You can link your PAN or Aadhaar by filling out a designated form at a branch or through an online service. In online banking, there is usually a KYC update section where you enter your PAN or Aadhaar number and upload document copies; once verified, the bank associates this information with your account.

  12. How can I check my account balance or get a statement through online banking?

    After logging in, your current account balance is typically displayed on the main page of your profile. To obtain a detailed statement, navigate to the “Account Statement” or “Transaction History” section, then select the account and date range. The system will generate a statement for the chosen period, which you can save as a PDF or print out. Additionally, if you wish, you can subscribe to electronic statements so the bank will send statements to your email on a regular basis.

  13. How do I transfer money online to another account?

    To transfer funds through online banking, log in and select the “Transfers” or “Payments” section. Then specify the type of transfer: either to an account within the same bank or to an account at another bank. If you are sending money to a new beneficiary, add them first by providing the required details (the beneficiary’s account number, the destination bank’s IFSC code, and the beneficiary’s name as it appears on their bank records). Once the beneficiary is added, enter the amount, choose the account from which the funds will be debited, and confirm the transaction using a one-time password (OTP) or other authentication method. Transfers between accounts in the same bank usually occur instantly, while interbank transfers are processed according to the rules of the chosen system: IMPS is executed almost immediately, NEFT in the next scheduled batch, and RTGS in real time during business hours (for amounts above a specified threshold).

  14. Are there any fees for online transfers?

    Transfers between accounts within the same bank are usually free of charge. When sending money to an account at a different bank (for example, via NEFT or RTGS), a small fee may apply according to the bank’s schedule of charges — typically a fixed amount or a percentage depending on the transfer amount. For IMPS transfers, the fee is either zero or very nominal. The exact charges depend on the bank’s policy and are periodically reviewed, so it’s advisable to check the current tariff schedule. Please note that international transfers have separate fees, which are generally higher.

  15. How can I send money abroad from my account?

    To make an international transfer (sending funds overseas), you will generally need to initiate the transaction at a bank branch. You’ll be required to provide the foreign beneficiary’s bank details: the beneficiary’s full name, their account number (or IBAN), the name and address of the beneficiary’s bank, and that bank’s SWIFT/BIC code. The bank will also ask you to complete a form (for example, Form A2 as required by the Reserve Bank of India) indicating the purpose of the transfer — such as tuition payment, medical expenses, etc. Once you have provided all the details and paid the fees, the bank will process the transfer; the funds typically reach the overseas bank within a few working days.

  16. Is there a tax on international transfers from Europe?

    Yes. Under Indian law, when receiving funds from abroad — particularly from foreign entities or companies — a tax known as TCS (Tax Collected at Source) is applicable. The current rate of TCS is 20% of the transferred amount. This is governed by Section 206C(1G) of the Indian Income Tax Act. The transfer cannot be processed until the applicable tax is paid. The responsibility for paying this tax lies with the recipient of the funds.

  17. What should I do if my bank card is lost or stolen?

    If you discover that your debit or credit card is missing, you should block it immediately. You can do this through online banking or the mobile app (in the card management section, there is usually a “Block Card” feature), or by calling the bank’s customer support. Acting quickly to block the card will prevent unauthorized use by anyone else. After blocking the card, contact the bank to request a replacement — they will issue you a new card with a different number, and the lost card will remain permanently blocked.
